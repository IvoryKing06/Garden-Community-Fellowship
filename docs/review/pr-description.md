# Phase 1 church website: pastor content and traditional visual redesign

Garden Community Fellowship’s Phase 1 website now presents the pastor-provided content in a traditional cream, navy, and burgundy design based on the owner’s visual reference. The reference supplies layout and styling only; all church information comes from approved content.

The homepage has a cream masthead with the approved motto, navy navigation with burgundy selected items, responsive dropdowns, a serif welcome headline, the complete approved oak/cross/sunrise banner, and Plan Your Visit / Learn About Us actions. Four highlights introduce service times, the purpose stated in the Articles of Faith, community, and involvement. Below them, a confirmed Celebrate Recovery feature, three recurring gatherings, and a Sunday visit card provide a clear next step. The same visual language extends across all public pages. Final polish removes the banner caption while retaining accessible alt text, dimensions, and responsive sizing; uses consistent Monday, Tuesday, and First Friday badges; and adds a prominent Google Maps Get Directions button on Plan Your Visit, opening the confirmed address in a new tab while retaining visitor email contact.

The complete pastor schedule, nine Statement of Faith paragraphs, and 19 expandable Articles of Faith are preserved. Sunday worship remains 10:00 AM–12:00 PM at 2931 Erskine Creek Rd, Lake Isabella, CA 93240. Public inquiries use gcf.online.studios@gmail.com. Donation, Discord, YouTube, and Twitch URLs are unchanged. Facebook, unapproved ministry nicknames, leadership details, document-only contact information, and tax ID remain excluded. No placeholder mockup dates, Michigan address, photographs, mission statement, or unapproved “Sermons in the Park” program is imported.

Content models, routing, prerendering, existing functionality, and the Phase 2 CMS plan remain intact. Step Study recurrence, a separate communion/potluck time, and café scheduling remain explicitly unresolved. Beliefs transcription awaits pastoral review before publication.

Validation: production build and lint pass; all nine Playwright tests pass with Chromium, including nine-route accessibility scans, responsive navigation and overflow, approved content and links, no-JavaScript HTML, full schedule access, homepage navigation, and desktop/mobile directions-link behavior. The Google Maps link’s URL parameters and new-tab behavior are verified with the external response intercepted; this does not claim validation of Google’s live service. Eighteen desktop/mobile screenshots are available in [the review gallery](https://github.com/IvoryKing06/Garden-Community-Fellowship/blob/feature/website-phase-1/docs/review/README.md).

No merge or deployment is included. Design approval is requested before publication.
